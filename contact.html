Contact FAQs

What happens during the initial discovery call?

It is a free 30 to 45-minute consultation where we discuss your goals, challenges, and determine if my program is the right fit for you.

When is the best time to reach out?

You can send an email anytime (I respond within 24 hours) or call during regular business hours. For scheduling, please reach out at least 48 hours in advance.

Are sessions available online?

Yes! I conduct online sessions globally via Zoom or Google Meet with the same depth and structure as in-person meetings.

What if I need to reschedule?

Life happens. Please notify me at least 24 hours prior to our scheduled session to reschedule without penalty.
